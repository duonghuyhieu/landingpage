Group finance · Web app

Badminton Fund 🏸

A ledger that talks back.

A fund-management platform for badminton groups: track players, sessions, cash flow and budgets in one place. The treasurer no longer digs through spreadsheets — just ask the AI assistant.

  • MembersProfile, status and balance for every player
  • SessionsLog each session and deduct costs automatically
  • LedgerTransparent income & expense history, instant lookup
  • BudgetAllocate court and shuttlecock costs by category

✦ AI inside — the Teppy assistant

Ask in plain Vietnamese, get answers from your real fund data:

  • “How much is left in the fund?”
  • “How much did the last session cost?”
  • “Who owes the fund the most?”

04Process

From idea to a product that ships.

  1. 1

    Listen

    Understand the problem, the users and the data you have. Find where AI truly makes a difference.

  2. 2

    Design

    Sketch the experience, conversation flows and system architecture. Agree on a clear scope.

  3. 3

    Build & integrate

    Develop in short iterations, with early working builds you can try and give feedback on.

  4. 4

    Run & optimise

    Deploy, monitor the quality of AI answers and optimise costs over time.
